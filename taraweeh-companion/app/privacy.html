Privacy Policy

Quran Verse Translator · Effective 1 October 2026

Quran Verse Translator listens to Quran recitation, finds the verse being recited and shows its translation. This page explains what data that involves and where it goes.

Microphone audio

  • The app uses the microphone only while you have pressed Start Listening.
  • Audio is streamed to our backend server, which forwards it to the speech-to-text provider you chose in Settings (Groq or OpenAI) using your own API key.
  • Neither the app nor our backend records or stores audio. The speech-to-text provider processes it under its own terms: Groq, OpenAI.

Your API key and settings

  • Your speech-to-text API key and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • The key is sent to our backend only while listening, so it can call your provider. The backend does not store it.

Diagnostic reports

  • Nothing is sent unless you tap Send to developer in Settings.
  • A report contains short transcripts of what was recognised and how the app decided which verse it was. It never contains audio or API keys.
  • Reports are held in server memory only, are capped at the 40 most recent, and are deleted whenever the server restarts.

Advertising

  • The free app shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. No ads are shown in Taraweeh mode.
  • AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address) and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses data.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iOS the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Children

The app is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.
