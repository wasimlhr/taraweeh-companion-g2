# v3.4.14 — reading controls and Even SDK update

The listening screen keeps the verse and simple Rakat controls visible, with
recognition diagnostics collapsed under Listening details. Settings adds
language search, saved favourites, separate phone/glasses language labels,
and a glasses layout preview.

Hold verse freezes the phone and glasses while recognition continues. Resume
shows the most recent result. Held pages can be read with swipes, double tap,
or the phone's navigation buttons; settings and language-pack repaints retain
the held page. The glasses menu provides start/stop, hold/resume, pronunciation
visibility, and Rakat corrections in Taraweeh mode.

Optional secondary brightness dims headers and pronunciation independently
of translation. The opt-in Arabic verse image prototype uses a bundled,
OFL-licensed Amiri Quran font, four 288 × 112 grayscale tiles, sequential SDK
transfers, and pagination. It shows Arabic on glasses instead of translation;
the phone retains its selected translation. Transfer failure restores a
readable native-text display. Physical-glasses transfer performance and
readability remain unverified; the option is labelled experimental.

Both dependency manifests pin Even SDK 0.0.16, including its duplicate timer
callback fix. Simulator 0.9.5 is pinned. The EHPK minimum Even App version is
2.2.10. App, server, Android and iOS version labels are aligned to 3.4.14.

Validation:

- 218 app/backend tests passed, plus 5 standalone phone build tests.
- All 82 language choices rendered through the real simulator and normal
  language/state handlers; 6,236 verses per output corpus were checked for
  missing firmware glyphs. Unsupported native scripts retain English fallback.
- Actual simulator menu selections exercised start, hold, resume,
  pronunciation switching and stop.
- Arabic pixels were verified in the simulator framebuffer, including held
  page 2 of 3 for Al-Baqarah 2:282.
- Phone-width and wide-layout captures, language search/favourites,
  per-device fallback labels, dim pronunciation and preview were checked.
- Controlled verse fixtures verify display, not real-speech recognition or
  physical hardware. Screenshots and machine-readable results are local under
  `language-evidence/ui-improvements/`; the reusable verifier is
  `scripts/verify-ui-simulator.js`.

Artifact: `taraweeh-companion-v3.4.14.ehpk`. Upload this package to update the
installed Even Hub app; updating Railway alone does not replace an EHPK.
